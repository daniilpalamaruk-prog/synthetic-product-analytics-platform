# Business cases

Existing analytical reports live in `docs/business_cases`. Each follows question → evidence → analysis → metric impact → interpretation → recommendation → limitations.

- Android incident: locate lower payment success on Android 3.7.0 and compare with the fixed version; do not hardcode the conclusion without calculation.
- Pricing: balance checkout conversion decline against ARPPU, revenue/contribution, retention where mature, and uncertainty.
- Acquisition: distinguish high registration volume/low CAC from cohort quality and contribution LTV/CAC; compare TikTok and Referral on generated evidence.
- Onboarding and retention: measure experiment/local effects separately from long-term cohort behavior.

Recommendations are proposals from one deterministic synthetic run. They are not real business impact or causal proof.
